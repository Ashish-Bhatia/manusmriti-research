# Case INVOCATORY-001

## Subject

Initial invocatory verse preceding Manu 1.1.

## Why selected

This is the first controlled textual-critical case because independent evidence reported by Patrick Olivelle records a disagreement between manuscript traditions.

## Evidence currently established

Olivelle states that:

- the verse is commented upon by Govinda
- the verse occurs in 18 northern manuscripts
- the verse is absent from the Southern manuscript tradition
- he considers the verse spurious

## Questions

1. What is the Sanskrit text of the verse?
2. Which 18 northern manuscripts contain it?
3. Which Southern manuscripts omit it?
4. What exactly does Govinda's commentary say?
5. Does Kullūka transmit it?
6. Does Medhātithi transmit it?
7. Does Mandlik print it?
8. Does Jolly print it?
9. Does Bühler translate it?
10. Does Jones translate it?
11. What does Olivelle print in the critical text?
12. What evidence does Olivelle give for rejection?
13. Is the verse an interpolation, a regional recension feature, an editorial addition, or something else?

## Current assessment

Not yet independently adjudicated.

## Evidence standard

Do not classify as interpolation solely because Olivelle calls it spurious. Record the underlying manuscript and commentary evidence before drawing an independent conclusion.
