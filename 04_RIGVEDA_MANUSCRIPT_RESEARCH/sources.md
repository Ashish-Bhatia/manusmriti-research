# Sources

## S1. Michael Witzel, Harvard Oriental Series

Michael Witzel, introduction to a Harvard Oriental Series volume on old Vedic manuscripts. The introduction states that a Rigveda manuscript at Benares Sanskrit University covers RV 8.6.25–10.191 and is dated 1361 CE. Footnote 3 gives `Samvat 1418; Cat. No. 40 XIV` and records Witzel's brief sighting in early 1973 while it was displayed in a glass case.

URL: https://list.indology.info/pipermail/indology/attachments/20231020/4e6355f0/attachment.pdf

Use: primary scholarly evidence for RV-001 through RV-005.

## S2. Verzeichnisse / Bibliographical Survey of Indian Manuscript Catalogues

The survey identifies the Benares Sanskrit College catalogue as a supplement to *The Pandit*, Vol. 3–10, Nos. 35A–109, 1869–1875, with approximately 3,000 manuscripts. It identifies the 1869 Vol. 3, No. 35A pages I–IV as the Rigveda section, approximately 100 manuscripts, followed by Rigveda/Yajurveda material.

URL: https://rep.adw-goe.de/bitstream/handle/11858/2359/2431_VOHD_Suppl_1.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=5

Use: catalogue structure and archival search target.

## S3. IGNCA, Saraswati Bhawan Library catalogue

IGNCA catalogue record A229/56093 is classified as Vedas > Rigveda > Vedic literature > Hinduism-Sacred books. The same A229 grouping contains records 56084–56097 with Vedic, Gṛhyaprayoga, Śrautaprayoga and Nirukta classifications.

URL: https://ignca.gov.in/mss/ms_sbl05.pdf

Use: modern catalogue evidence for RV-009 and RV-010.

## S4. IGNCA-hosted historical manuscript material

An IGNCA-hosted historical source records a manuscript dated Vikrama Samvat 1418 as corresponding to A.D. 1361–62, supporting the chronological interpretation of Samvat 1418.

URL: https://ignca.gov.in/Asi_data/18230.pdf

Use: independent chronological context only. It does not identify the Benares Rigveda manuscript.

## Research rule

A source is not treated as proving object identity unless it explicitly connects the historical and modern identifiers or provides a sufficiently unique physical/textual description to establish the connection.
