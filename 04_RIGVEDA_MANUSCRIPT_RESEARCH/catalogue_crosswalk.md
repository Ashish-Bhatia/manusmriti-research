# Catalogue Crosswalk

## Historical record

Witzel gives the early Benares Rigveda manuscript as:

- Repository: Benares Sanskrit University
- Text: RV 8.6.25–10.191
- Date: 1361 CE
- Samvat: 1418
- Catalogue: `Cat. No. 40 XIV`

The historical Benares Sanskrit College manuscript catalogue was published as a supplement to *The Pandit* from 1869 to 1875. The bibliographical survey identifies it as *Rājakīya-Vārāṇasī-vidyāmandira-Sarasvatibhavana-varti-pustakānām Sūcipatram*.

The first 1869 issue relevant to this investigation is:

- *The Pandit*, Vol. 3, No. 35A, 1869
- pp. I–IV: Rigveda manuscripts, approximately 100 entries
- pp. V–XII: Rigveda and Yajurveda material

## Modern record

IGNCA's Saraswati Bhawan Library catalogue contains:

- Repository code: A229
- Accession/record: 56093
- Classification: Vedas > Rigveda > Vedic literature > Hinduism-Sacred books

The same A229 cluster includes records 56084–56097 covering Vedic literature, Gṛhyaprayoga, Śrautaprayoga and Nirukta.

## Required documentary bridge

A positive identification requires one or more of:

1. Old catalogue number `40 XIV` in the modern catalogue.
2. Samvat 1418 or 1361 date in the modern manuscript record.
3. Explicit textual range RV 8.6.25–10.191.
4. A former shelfmark/accession number linking the old and modern records.
5. A microfilm card or register linking the object to the historical catalogue.
6. Physical-description agreement plus a documented accession chain.

## Search target

The highest-value archival target remains the actual four-page Rigveda section of the 1869 Benares catalogue. The search should locate entry 40 and inspect all columns, abbreviations, folio counts, textual incipits/excerpts, dates, script/material and provenance notes.
