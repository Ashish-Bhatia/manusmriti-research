# Rigveda Manuscript Research

## Objective

Reconstruct the provenance and catalogue history of the early Rigveda manuscript reported by Michael Witzel as:

- Textual range: RV 8.6.25–10.191
- Date: 1361 CE
- Vikrama Samvat: 1418
- Historical repository: Benares Sanskrit University
- Historical catalogue reference: Cat. No. 40 XIV
- Physical sighting reported by Witzel: early 1973, displayed in a glass case

The principal unresolved task is to map the historical `Cat. No. 40 XIV` reference to a modern Saraswati Bhawan / IGNCA accession or microfilm record.

## Current conclusion

The 1361 manuscript is independently documented. IGNCA record A229/56093 is independently identified as a Rigveda manuscript. No direct source recovered so far proves that A229/56093 is the same physical manuscript as Witzel's Cat. No. 40 XIV.

Therefore this repository deliberately records the identity as **unproven**, rather than converting a plausible catalogue coincidence into a fact.

## Research priorities

1. Recover the 1869 Rigveda catalogue section in *The Pandit*, Vol. 3, No. 35A, pp. I–IV.
2. Locate historical catalogue entry 40 XIV.
3. Extract its complete descriptive metadata.
4. Search later Benares/Saraswati Bhawan catalogues for the old reference.
5. Establish the crosswalk to modern A229/accession/microfilm identifiers.
6. Compare textual beginning/end, date, script, material, foliation and colophon data.
7. Preserve every claim with a source and confidence assessment.

## Evidence standard

- Confirmed: directly stated by a primary or specialist scholarly source.
- Strong: supported by independent institutional/bibliographical evidence but missing one direct cross-reference.
- Candidate: plausible match requiring documentary confirmation.
- Unproven: no sufficient evidence.

## Key source anchors

- Michael Witzel, Harvard Oriental Series introduction, describing the 1361 Benares Rigveda manuscript and Cat. No. 40 XIV.
- G. Bühler / bibliographical catalogue history of Benares Sanskrit College manuscripts, documenting the 1869–75 catalogue and its Rigveda section.
- IGNCA, Saraswati Bhawan Library catalogue, identifying A229/56093 as a Rigveda manuscript.
