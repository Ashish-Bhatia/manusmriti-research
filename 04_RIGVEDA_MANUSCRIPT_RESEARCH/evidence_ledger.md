## Interpretation

The strongest identity chain is:

`RV 8.6.25–10.191` + `Samvat 1418` + `1361 CE` + `Benares Sanskrit University` + `Cat. No. 40 XIV`.

A modern A229 Rigveda record exists, but the documentary bridge from `40 XIV` to `A229/56093` has not been established.

## Negative finding

Do not cite A229/56093 as the 1361 manuscript unless a catalogue, accession register, microfilm record, colophon, or other direct documentary source establishes the cross-reference.
