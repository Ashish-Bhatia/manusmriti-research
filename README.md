# Manusmriti Research

A source-critical investigation of the Manava-Dharmashastra (Manusmriti).

## Objective

Reconstruct the text, transmission, interpretation, translation history, historical use, and modern reception using primary evidence and explicit uncertainty.

## Core distinction

Manuscript evidence != critical edition != commentary != translation != historical practice != modern interpretation.

## Research tracks

1. Primary Sanskrit text and manuscript tradition
2. Commentaries and recensions
3. Translation history
4. Dharmasutra and Dharmashastra comparanda
5. Vedic, epic, Buddhist and Jain comparanda
6. Historical practice and material evidence
7. Colonial construction of Hindu law
8. Modern reception
9. Controversial verses
10. Interpolation and textual corruption

## Research standard

Every significant conclusion must be traceable to identifiable evidence. Competing evidence must be recorded.

See `RESEARCH_METHOD.md`.
