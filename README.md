# Manusmriti Research Repository

This repository stores the source corpus and research records for the Manusmriti project, with supporting manuscript-history research.

## Current corpus

- `01_MANUSMRITI_PRIMARY/` — primary editions and Sanskrit source texts
- `02_COMMENTARIES/` — traditional and scholarly commentaries
- `03_TRANSLATIONS/` — historical translations
- `04_RIGVEDA_MANUSCRIPT_RESEARCH/` — early Rigveda manuscript provenance investigation

## Rigveda investigation

The current research focuses on a documented 1361 CE Benares Rigveda manuscript covering RV 8.6.25–10.191. Michael Witzel identifies it as Samvat 1418 and gives the historical catalogue reference `Cat. No. 40 XIV`.

The modern IGNCA Saraswati Bhawan catalogue separately lists A229/56093 as a Rigveda manuscript. The repository records this as a candidate modern record, not as a proven identity match.

## Research standard

Every manuscript-identity claim must be traceable to documentary evidence. Search-engine results are discovery aids. They are not treated as proof of physical-object identity.

The next decisive archival target is the Rigveda section of the 1869 Benares Sanskrit College catalogue published in *The Pandit*, Vol. 3, No. 35A, pp. I–IV. The objective is to recover entry `40 XIV` and trace it through subsequent Benares/Saraswati Bhawan accession and microfilm records.
