## Important methodological distinction

The following are separate analytical categories:

1. Scribal error
2. Scribal omission
3. Scribal addition
4. Recensional variation
5. Deliberate textual alteration
6. Editorial reconstruction
7. Commentary
8. Translation error
9. Translation choice
10. Interpretive interpolation
11. Modern ideological reinterpretation

No category is to be substituted for another without evidence.

## Current conclusion

The existence of textual variation is established.

The existence of deliberate textual changes in the transmission history is established as a general textual-critical phenomenon.

The claim that any particular controversial verse was deliberately inserted remains an individual evidentiary question.
